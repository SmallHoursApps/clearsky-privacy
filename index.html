Clear Sky — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Clear Sky is a watch face for Garmin watches, published by Small Hours Apps (smallhourslabs@gmail.com).

What Clear Sky stores

Only your face settings (rain threshold, data fields, time colour), which Garmin syncs through the Garmin Connect / Connect IQ app.

What Clear Sky collects

Nothing. It requests no permissions, makes no network requests, has no analytics or ads, and sends no data to the publisher or any third party. Weather comes from the forecast your watch already receives from Garmin; Clear Sky only reads it on the watch.

Deleting your data

Removing Clear Sky from your watch removes its settings.
